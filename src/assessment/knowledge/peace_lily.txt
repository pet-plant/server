Plant knowledge for visual assessment only

Species: Spathiphyllum wallisii
Common name: Peace lily

Healthy visual traits:
- Leaves are usually upright to gently arching.
- Leaves are usually broad, smooth, and mostly even dark green.
- A healthy plant should not show widespread yellowing, browning, dry edges, or collapsed posture.

Common visible stress cues:
- Drooping, limp, or collapsed leaves.
- Yellowing leaves or yellow patches.
- Brown leaf tips or brown leaf edges.
- Dry, curled, wilted, or damaged-looking leaf edges.

Use this knowledge only to interpret visible traits for this species.
Do not diagnose biological causes such as underwatering, overwatering, disease, pests, nutrient deficiency, or root problems.
Do not give care advice.
